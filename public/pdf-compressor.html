Two honest methods

Lossless rewrites PDF objects and preserves text, links, forms and vectors, but savings can be small. Raster redraws each page as JPEG for stronger reduction.

Raster trade-offs

Raster output loses selectable text, links, forms, layers, signatures and accessibility structure. It is best for scans or share copies, not archival masters.
