What does the score mean?

This is a rule-based resume readiness estimate, not an employer's ATS score or a prediction of selection. We check extractable text, contact details, common sections, evidence of experience and document length. Job keyword coverage is reported separately.

Scanned image PDFs need OCR before they can be checked. Layout, columns and visual reading order are not fully assessed by text extraction. Review the extracted text before relying on the report.
